About Our School

Established in 1953, Phoubakchao Makha High School is a Government school serving students from Pre-Primary to Class X. The school is committed to providing accessible education and supporting the overall development of its students.

Our school community includes dedicated teaching staff, students, parents and local stakeholders working together to create a positive learning environment.

Headmaster: Md Abdul Samad (State Awardee)

Established: 1953

School Facilities & Support

Key facilities and student support provided by the Government school

📚 Free Textbooks

Free textbooks are provided to eligible students as per Government provisions.

👕 Free Uniform

🍲 Mid-Day Meal
